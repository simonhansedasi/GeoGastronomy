# Out-of-state regulatory screen (2026-10-07)

Web-search screen only. Most figures come from consultant sites, not statute or regulator pages. Verify against the regulator before relying on any number. Q: where can an outdoor/greenhouse farm site finder be sold to someone who can legally use it?

## Top Shelf Data
Washington only, built on public WSLCB data (MJBizDaily directory, topshelfdata.com). A co-founder said a nationwide model isn't possible because other states don't publish the data. Not found: any expansion since. Treat it as irrelevant to out-of-state work unless the site says otherwise. Alternatives to look at: Headset, CannaIQ, CannMenus (27 states, menu/price data).

## What this says
- Every recently legalized state has a license cap or closed window. A site finder only has customers where licenses are still open or where winners need sites.
- Delaware is the clearest open lane (outdoor tiers, 60 slots, vacant licenses) but is very small. Virginia's 2027 application window is the next real opening and is the one worth positioning for.
- Minnesota's lottery winners (50) are the real prospects there. They already hold licenses and need sites. Their locations are a short, researchable list.
- Climate is not the bottleneck. Humid Mid-Atlantic and Midwest sites are mold-limited, so VPD and humidity features carry over but the California cluster definition does not.
- No enforcement-style validation target exists in any of these states. Candidate substitutes: published license/premises addresses, hemp acreage, or agronomic suitability proxies.
